## Current producer/consumer contract

Accounting.Data/InvoiceCreationDownstreamClients.cs currently sends GET detail, full PUT `{InvoiceId,Accepted:true,...financial fields}` with expected ModifiedDate, then PUT decision `{Accepted:true}` with its operation UUID. It does not pass InvoiceId or EmployeeInitiated to decision. Invoice is already committed before completion; document/file/notification steps follow. Those side effects are excluded.

Quotation ordinary PUT rejects a first Accepted promotion with409. On a historical accepted root it can attach an invoice and change ModifiedDate without binding DecisionOrderVersion. Approved late decision attachment instead atomically saves invoice plus the pre-attachment version binding and preserves prior acceptance/outcome/provenance. Linked-order keys include that bound version, otherwise ModifiedDate/CreatedDate.

Intranet `40275ec6c5b3547ab6d5363fcb8cc03b82d3f473` sends BFF invoice creation to Accounting with operation UUID and optional scoped invoice-create delegation. Accounting downstream completion uses normal workload authentication. That delegation admits invoice creation only; it is NOT an approved Accounting-to-Quotation employee override. Accounting workload must remain denied for explicit EmployeeInitiated intent. No machine allowlist, actor headers, signed-claim fallback, fabricated employee identity or new grants are proposed.
